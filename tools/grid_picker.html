Grid picker

Click the four corners of the Hoddle Grid. The other 41 intersections are interpolated from them, exactly as grid.py does it.

1  Pick the corners

Zoom in until you can see the intersection clearly, then click the middle of it.

2  Check the fit

The grid draws once all four corners are in. Every line should sit on a real street. If one drifts, undo and re-click that corner.

The plain basemap hides shop and restaurant icons. Turn on detail only if you need a landmark to find an intersection.

3  Overlay your sensors

Load data/sensors.csv to see which blocks fall inside the match radius.

Red blocks have no sensor within the radius. They route on a penalty, not a measurement.
